1SOLIDS, LIQUIDS, AND GASES
All matter in the universe is made of microscopic particles. The arrangement and motion of these particles dictate whether a
material is a solid, liquid, or gas.
2CHANGES OF STATE & ENERGY TRANSITIONS
Phase changes are physical transformations caused by adding or removing thermal energy (heat). Kinetic energy alters
particle movement without changing chemical identity.
Key Rule: Thermal energy directly increases the kinetic speed of particles. When heat energy overcomes intermolecular
attractive forces, a state change occurs. Temperature stays constant during a phase transition!

3THE PLANETARY WATER CYCLE
Earth's water cycle is a massive global heat engine demonstrating phase changes on a planetary scale powered by solar
radiation.
4ATOMS, ELEMENTS & THE PERIODIC TABLE
An atom is the smallest fundamental unit of matter. An element is a pure substance consisting of only one type of atom.

5COMPOUNDS, FORMULAE & MIXTURES
Matter combines chemically or physically. Understanding the boundary between compounds and mixtures is fundamental to
chemistry.
DIAGNOSTIC MATRIX: COMPOUNDS VS. MIXTURES
Feature Profile Chemical Compound Physical Mixture
Composition Elements bonded in strict fixed ratios (e.g., CO2) Substances mixed in any arbitrary proportion
Chemical PropertiesCompletely distinct from parent elements Retains individual properties of components
Separation MethodRequires chemical reaction / electrolysis Separable via filtration, evaporation, magnets
Energy Input Heat/light often absorbed/released during formationNo chemical energy exchange during mixing
Examples Pure Water (H2O), Salt (NaCl) Air, Saltwater, Sand + Iron filings
